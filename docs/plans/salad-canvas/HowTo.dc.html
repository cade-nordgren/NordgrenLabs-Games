How to Play

Six rounds. Avoid points. Lowest total wins.

The idea

Each round has one thing to avoid. Every card is dealt out, you play tricks, and whatever you collect may cost you points. After six rounds the player with the fewest points wins.

The six rounds

1No Tricks10 a trickWin 3 tricks: 30 points.
2No Hearts10 a heartCollect 4 hearts: 40 points.
3No Queens25 a queenCollect the Q♠︎ and Q♦︎: 50 points.
4No King of Spades100Whoever takes the K♠︎ scores 100.
5No Last Trick100Win the final trick of the round: 100.
6Canadian Saladall fiveTricks, hearts, queens, K♠︎ and last trick all count at once.

Playing a trick

Lead. The player clockwise of the dealer leads the first trick with any card. Whoever wins a trick leads the next one.

Follow suit. If you hold the suit that was led, you must play it. Cards you can play rise up; the rest are greyed and locked. If you have none, play anything.

Win the trick. The highest card of the suit that was led wins. Aces are high. There are no trumps, so another suit never wins.

More than one deck

With 2 or 3 decks there are identical cards. If two of the same card tie for highest, the one played last wins.

When the cards don't divide evenly, the spare ones form a face-down kitty. The winner of the first trick takes it, face up for everyone, and its cards score like any other.

Final scores

After round 6 the totals count up round by round. Lowest total wins. Players on the same total share the same place.
