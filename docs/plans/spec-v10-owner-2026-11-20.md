# Owner specification v10 (received 2026-11-20, six gameplay interpretations approved)

The owner's approval of six behaviours that were already implemented and carried "to confirm" remainders after the relabel of 2026-11-20 (plan v11, commit `73440e9`): the Strength Ring at full HP (D47), no monster card for the Thunder Ring (D49), the small flask's free-use timing (D53), another hero entering the lair while the dragon is awake (D40), the jump back across the well (D42) and the Protection Ring's own timing (D50). Where this file disagrees with an earlier spec or register ruling, this file wins. Owner text is quoted verbatim in the block; do not reinterpret it.

This is not an approval of the catalogue or the game: every card stays `draft`. What was done with it: each behaviour was checked against every bullet below, in the code and in `tests/keep/spec-v10.test.js`; all six already matched, so no rule changed and MECHANICS_VERSION stays `rb-0.2/sp-1.0/v10`. The parts whose precise implementation is not printed are relabelled OWNER_APPROVED_INTERPRETATION (dated 2026-11-20, spec v10) in plan section 9 (D40, D42, D47, D49, D50, D53), the matrix, section 13, the audit's status tables, RING_1 to RING_4 and the four small flask cards, each keeping the printed mechanic apart from the approved interpretation. D42's well tile that is not straight is not covered and stays as it was. The remaining open points are inventoried in [`unresolved-gameplay-2026-11-20.md`](unresolved-gameplay-2026-11-20.md).

> Owner decision: APPROVED — Six outstanding gameplay interpretations
>
> I approve the six previously implemented behaviors, with the following clarifications.
>
> 1. Strength Ring — APPROVED
>
> The Strength Ring may be used even when the hero is at full starting HP. Skip the hero's turn. Gain exactly 5 HP. Allow HP to exceed starting HP. Consume the ring. Do not increase the Strength attribute.
>
> 2. Thunder Ring — APPROVED
>
> During an ordinary monster encounter, the Thunder Ring immediately causes the monster to flee. Do not draw a Monster reaction card if the ring successfully resolves the encounter. Consume the ring. Do not offer it during ambushes or surprise attacks. No A/B/C combat occurs when the ring successfully drives the monster away.
>
> 3. Small Flask — APPROVED WITH TIMING CLARIFICATION
>
> Drinking a flask does not consume the hero's turn. Allow drinking during the hero's normal action window. Do not allow it to interrupt an active encounter, trap resolution, combat exchange, or other unresolved event. Roll D12 and D6 and apply the net HP change as already implemented. Allow HP above starting HP. If HP falls to 0 or below, the hero dies. Return the used flask to its appropriate source deck using the established card lifecycle.
>
> The free-action timing is an owner-approved interpretation, not an explicitly printed turn-cost rule.
>
> 4. Entering the Dragon's Lair while awake — APPROVED
>
> Another hero may enter the Lair while the dragon is awake. No treasure or Dragon cards are drawn while it remains awake. Entering does not reset the dragon. Preserve the existing forced-exit and dragon-reset rulings.
>
> 5. Reverse well jump — APPROVED
>
> A hero who has successfully crossed a well may attempt to jump back. Apply the same Agility test and applicable modifiers. On success, move to the original side of the well tile. End the turn. On failure, apply the normal well-jump consequences. Do not create additional passages or movement options.
>
> 6. Protection Ring timing — APPROVED
>
> When a qualifying trap occurs: Identify the trap and offer the ring before resolving its consequences. The hero may use the ring or decline. If used, consume it and cancel that trap's consequences. If declined, retain the ring and resolve the trap normally. Do not allow retroactive use after damage or another consequence has already resolved.
>
> Preserve the existing approved trap eligibility list, including the Rotating Room. Wizard's Curse remains excluded under its separately documented interpretation.
>
