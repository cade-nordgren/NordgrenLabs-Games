# Audit: the owner's card catalogue against the keep engine (2026-11-19)

Source: the owner's catalogue package of 2026-11-19 (implementation brief, `drakborgen_master_card_data.json`, card inventory and per-deck photo catalogues), outside the repo. The package's mechanical summaries are the owner's paraphrases; here every card is stored as structured effect data in `site/keep/content/original/*.js`, and the words players read are generated by `engine/describe.js`. No card wording, art or biography is copied. Card display names are the owner's short English labels.

Stage 1 (this section) is the audit before code: what the engine already supported, what was missing, and every ambiguity. Section 5 is the final status table, written at the end (stage 6).

Mechanics the cards need that the engine could not express:

| Card(s) | Needed | Minimal general addition (cards stay data) |
|---|---|---|
| Small flask (5 copies over 4 decks) | kept consumable; roll D12 and D6 together, gain the D12, lose the D6 | `ADJUST_HP { amount }` (a net change: a gain heals, a loss is damage), expression term `minusReg`, use timing `free` (on the hero's own turn, does not use the turn), `afterUse: 'bottom'` (exists) |
| Poisonous mist, Explosion | skip N turns, N fixed or one per damage point | `DEAL_DAMAGE.store` (damage actually taken), `SKIP_TURNS { turns: Expr, label }` (a condition whose turn start ends the turn) |
| Trapdoor + Rope | Luck test, D6 fall, climb-out Agility test on later turns; the rope frees without the test | existing `CHECK_STAT` + `SET_CONDITION` with `onTurnStart` (a `REQUEST_CHOICE` "Climb out" then the test); new card field `frees: [conditionId]` on a kept item; new `SET_CONDITION.label` for the stuck state's words |
| Torch goes out, Giant spider | name three different D6 numbers, roll, branch on a match | `NAME_NUMBERS { count, store?, onMatch, onMiss }` and the action `NAME_NUMBERS { numbers: '1,3,5' }` |
| Giant spider | Fight or Flee, flee back the normal way | `FLEE` op; `REQUEST_CHOICE` option `requires: 'can-flee'` |
| Ceiling collapses | name one number; a match is death | `NAME_NUMBERS { count: 1 }`, `KILL_HERO` |
| Wizard's curse | D6 table, rotate every placed yellow corridor tile | existing `ROLL_TABLE`; `ROTATE_TILES { mark, degrees: 90 / 180 / 270 }` |
| Bones | D12, odd = Skeleton encounter | `ROLL_TABLE` row `{ parity: 'odd' / 'even' }` |
| Chest, Sarcophagus, Fallen warrior | optional draw | existing `REQUEST_CHOICE` + `DRAW_CARD` (no new op) |
| Hazards that are not traps (snakes, bats, centipede, scorpion, ...) | a kind that is not "trap" | kind `event` (room and other decks); kind `reshuffle` in the room and door decks |

Not needed (already expressible): `D12 - Agility`, `D12 - Armour`, `D12 - 3 - Armour` (`{ die, plus, minusStat }`), `D6 - 2`, fixed HP loss (integer amount), treasure (`gold` + `RETAIN_HELD`), monster rows with fight HP and strike expressions.

## 3. Ambiguities and how each is handled

Every interpretation is a register row (plan section 9, D51-D66). Items marked **NEEDS OWNER CONFIRMATION** are entered with the most literal reading and say so in the card's notes.

1. **FP in the HP position** on four monster cells: #6 Flee/Skeleton, #14 Flee/Orc, #15 Flee/Dark Elf, #15 Flee/Skeleton. Read as HP (the fight needs a value). NEEDS OWNER CONFIRMATION (D51).
2. **Monster combat B/C letters** inferred from the hit targets in the photos (the package's caveat). Entered as inferred (D52).
3. **Treasure chamber coin multiplicity**: one card per denomination 100-300 (21 cards) is inferred from the total of 33. NEEDS OWNER CONFIRMATION (D64).
4. **Ceiling collapses**: the official 2023 rulebook was checked (text only) and says nothing about this card (its section 8 says the instructions are on the cards now). Most literal reading: name one number, roll one D6; a match is death, otherwise damage equal to that roll. Alternative: a second D6 for the damage. NEEDS OWNER CONFIRMATION (D59).
5. **Door deck "shuffle again"**: the deck is reshuffled (R62); the door does not open and is handled as "stays shut" (another way may be tried this turn). Alternative: draw another door card. NEEDS OWNER CONFIRMATION (D62).
6. **Torch card after relighting**: the card says keep it; no discard is printed, so it stays held (it goes back only at death, D28). NEEDS OWNER CONFIRMATION (D56).
7. **Flask**: the two dice are applied as one net change; a net gain stops at starting HP (D04: the card does not say otherwise); a net loss is damage and can kill. Drinking does not use the turn and is offered on the hero's own turn when choosing an action or a way on, not in an encounter (D53).
8. **Trapdoor and rope**: after the climb-out test passes (or the rope frees the hero) the turn goes on as a normal turn (D55). The rope is never consumed (the card does not say so).
9. **Giant spider**: Flee is offered only when a way back exists (as for monsters, RB 8.6 / 2023 rules 9.4); a successful flee is the normal flight; a kill on a later turn ends that turn (D58).
10. **Skip turns**: the hero's next N turns pass; zero damage from the mist skips nothing (D54).
11. **Wizard's curse**: every placed yellow corridor tile turns; heroes stay on their squares; fallen portcullises (board edges) stay; no special tile is yellow in the owner's tile data (D57).
12. **Trap classification** (Protection Ring, D50): marked `kind: 'trap'` only where the catalogue names a trap: Arrow trap, Trapdoor, Door trap (Luck), Door trap (D6), chest Trap. Not marked (question for the owner): Poisonous snakes, Poisonous mist, Explosion, Ceiling collapses, Wizard's curse, Vampire bats (both), Giant spider, Torch goes out, Centipede, Scorpion (D63).
13. **Optional draws** (chest, sarcophagus, fallen warrior): the 2023 rulebook says room cards sometimes let the hero choose to try their luck in other decks (box after 8.1), so the draw is a choice (D61).
14. **Reshuffle cards** in five decks: existing R62 (own pile only, used cards go back under their own deck); not reopened.
15. Package section L items already settled: 4 (Protection Ring scope) D50 plus item 12 above; 5 (Strength Ring cap) D47; 6 (ring timings) D47-D50; 7 (reshuffle) R62; 8 (biographies) hero `text` stays empty.

## 4. What changed (stages 2-5)

- **Content (all `draft`):** `monster.js` 15 cards (180 cells), `combat.js` + 12 monster cards (S09-S12, `groupName`), `room.js` 60 (27 variants), `treasury.js` 33, `room-search.js` 14, `search.js` 15, `door.js` 15, `chest.js` 14, `dragon.js` 8 (data entry only; the dragon rules are unchanged). Each card's notes cite its catalogue id and any register row. Tiles, board, chamber, heroes, hero combat cards, crossbows and rings untouched.
- **Engine (general ops, no per-card code):** `ADJUST_HP`, `SKIP_TURNS`, `NAME_NUMBERS` (+ action), `KILL_HERO`, `FLEE`, `ROTATE_TILES`, `DEAL_DAMAGE.store`, Expr `minusReg`, `ROLL_TABLE` parity rows, `REQUEST_CHOICE` option `requires: 'can-flee'`, `SET_CONDITION.label`, card `frees`, use timing `free` (`turn.resume`), door kind `reshuffle`. `MECHANICS_VERSION` v7 (older saves refused).
- **Schema and checker:** kinds `event`/`reshuffle`; value checks (treasure gold and kept, chamber cards are treasure, shuffle cards shuffle, usable or freeing items kept, a complete monster deck covers every type); the checker lists entries marked NEEDS OWNER CONFIRMATION.
- **Play-test:** with every slot written it runs on owner data only, under "Play-test: your own draft content, not approved yet" (Details: draft categories, entries to confirm). A category falling back to fixtures keeps the NON-AUTHENTIC banner. Full Start blocked until approval.
- **UI:** name-the-numbers picker (six toggles, Roll the D6) with the roll card; optional-draw prompts ("Open the chest?"); reason lines for lasting effects on the hero and the prompt ("Unconscious: 2 turns to miss", "Torch out", "In a trapdoor pit"); a notice for each skipped turn; flask Use buttons (also while choosing whether to keep moving) with both dice on the roll card; card tables (curse, bones, spider flight) on the roll card with their result line; the board redraws rotated corridors from state; monster type names in the fight row; the rope listed as kept ("works by itself"). Card text everywhere now comes from `describe.js`.
- **Tests:** `tests/keep/catalogue.test.js` (42 tests) plus updates to `spec-v5`, `content` and `kit` tests for the now-complete content.
