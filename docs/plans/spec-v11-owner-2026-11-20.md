> Owner decision — Gameplay rulings and final audit
>
> I've reviewed the nine remaining gameplay decisions against the official 2023 Drakborgen rules and our existing owner interpretations.
>
> Approve decisions 1–5 and 9 as currently implemented.
>
> 1. Trapdoor: A successful climb allows the turn to continue. Rope permits climbing without an Agility test and is not consumed.
> 2. Giant Spider: Preserve the special combat procedure, successful flight on D6 results 3–6, and turn-ending behavior after a later successful fight.
> 3. Wizard's Curse: Heroes remain on their corridor tiles when those tiles rotate. Their available exits change with the tile orientation. Classify this positioning rule as an owner-approved interpretation.
> 4. Mist: Skip one turn per point of damage actually received. Explosion: Skip the following turn.
> 5. Dragon forced exit: Choose an adjacent, already-placed maze tile. Preserve our previous handling when no destination exists. This is an owner ruling for the edge case.
> 6. Rule switches: Leave experimental switches without user-facing controls during the current play-test. Preserve their documented defaults.
>
> Decision 6 — APPROVED WITH CLARIFICATION
> When a dragon forces a surviving hero onto a special tile, apply that tile's applicable entry effects.
> However, do not draw a Room card during this forced movement. Section 10.1 explicitly excludes Room draws.
> Document applying other special tile effects as an owner-approved interpretation.
>
> Decision 7 — APPROVED WITH CLARIFICATION
> When a hero successfully flees from a monster back into the Dragon's Lair, treat this as entering that special room.
> * If the dragon is asleep, resolve the normal Lair treasure and Dragon draw.
> * If the dragon is awake, do not draw treasure or Dragon cards.
> * Preserve the existing awakening, treasure-loss and reset mechanics.
> * Do not grant an additional ordinary movement action.
> Document this as the approved interpretation of sections 9.4 and 10.
>
> Decision 8 — NOT YET APPROVED
> Do not automatically approve the rule that a closed door ends the turn during every forced movement.
> Section 6.2 permits choosing another available exit when an ordinary door stays closed. A door trap ends the turn.
> Please identify the exact forced-movement situation, its triggering rule, whether another exit is legal, and the existing engine behavior. Present a proposed resolution without changing the implementation yet.
>

> * Equal highest treasure value results in co-winners — owner-approved tie interpretation.
> * Successfully relighting the torch allows that turn to continue — supported by the card instructions.
