# Owner research: 2023 rulebook statements (received 2026-11-19)

The owner supplied a research report on the 2023 edition. This file records its **rule statements** in our own words, as a checklist for the engine. It is not content: the report's hero names, stat lines and other card-level values are deliberately not recorded here and are not imported into the game (plan section 2: content slots take the owner's own entries).

Source the report cites as highest authority: the publisher's official eight-page 2023 Swedish rulebook, `https://algaspel.se/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/38010503_Drakborgen_rules.pdf`. Until a statement below is checked against that PDF it is tagged "owner research"; once checked it may be tagged A (`explicit2023`). Owner rulings in `spec-v2` and `spec-v3` still win where the owner chose to diverge.

## Component counts (structure only)

89 labyrinth tiles including the double chamber tile (so 88 maze tiles); 8 hero cards; 2 special-weapon cards; 36 combat cards (3 per hero = 24, plus 12 monster combat cards, read as 4 monster types x 3); 33 treasure-chamber cards; 8 dragon cards (7 asleep, 1 awake); 60 room; 15 search; 14 room-search; 14 chest; 15 door; 15 monster cards; 4 rings. The 15 monster cards are cards, not 15 monster species: each card has rows per monster type and a column per hero choice, giving the reaction and, where a fight results, the HP.

## Rule statements to check against the engine

**Checked 2026-11-19** against the official PDF (read as text, rule statements only): all 19 are confirmed as worded, with these notes. 2: 2023 rules 2, 5.2 and 8. 7: section 11. 9: 9.5 prints "T12-TF" only; the floor at 0 is ruling D03. 12: the outcome of a failed jump is not printed (D08). 17: 10.1. 19: section 2, "any tower" before sunset. Component counts: contents list; the 24 + 12 combat split from 1.7 and 9.3. The engine audit (verdicts, rows, tests) is plan section 7.1.

1. **Yellow corridor.** Not searchable. Entering one draws no room card and lets the hero continue at once to an adjacent square through a free passage in the same turn.
2. **Room card on every entry.** A room card is drawn whenever a hero enters a black- or red-dot tile, including one already on the board.
3. **Searching.** Only in black- or red-dot rooms, instead of moving; repeatable on later turns. Uses the room-search deck.
4. **Secret passage.** A found passage lets the hero cross a wall once; it is temporary and blocked again after use. Found passages, lifted portcullises and opened doors are all temporary free passages.
5. **Flee.** Retreat is to the tile the hero just came from. If that route cannot be used (the rulebook's example: a secret passage just used), Flee is not offered and the hero must Attack or Wait. A successful flee draws no room card, but a special room's rule on the destination still applies. A monster card may give a hit before a successful flight, or a hit followed by forced combat, with damage such as D6 - 2.
6. **Encounter commitment.** Attack / Wait / Flee is committed before the monster card is drawn. The player to the hero's right is the monster player. A special weapon is a fourth declaration; the card defines its effect and the monster reacts as if the hero chose Attack.
7. **Monster HP.** In multiplayer the monster player does not reveal HP; damage is tracked privately and death is announced. **In a one-player game the rulebook says the monster's HP is not secret** and the fight uses the physical dice-target system. (This differs from D37, which the owner approved: see the open question in the plan.)
8. **A/B/C combat.** Secret simultaneous choice; same letter costs both 1; A beats B, B beats C, C beats A; loser takes 1, or 2 where the winning card states a double hit; repeat until one side dies.
9. **Ambush.** First damage of D12 minus Luck (minimum 0), then only Attack or Flee, then the monster card.
10. **Doors.** Trying a door draws a door card. Opens: movement continues through it. Stays closed: the hero may use another free passage the same turn. Door trap: the turn ends. An opened door relocks after passing, except that a hero who meets a monster at once, flees and succeeds gets back through it.
11. **Portcullis.** A red-dot room drops a portcullis behind the entering hero; entering needs no Strength test. Crossing that edge later tests Strength; success crosses and it closes again; failure ends the turn.
12. **Well.** A failed first Agility test kills; survival leaves the hero at the edge. Crossing on a later turn tests Agility with +1 to the roll per treasure-chamber card carried; cards may be discarded before the roll.
13. **Rotating room.** Turns 90 degrees clockwise every time someone enters; not when someone merely stays.
14. **Rubble.** On entry the hero stops at the entrance and the turn ends; on a later turn the hero retreats or tests Agility to force through and then picks an exit.
15. **Dark room.** Discovery ends the turn; next turn a D6 picks the exit, and the hero then continues that turn normally.
16. **Chamber.** Entering either centre square: take two treasure-chamber cards privately, then draw one dragon card. A sleeping card is set aside, not returned, so the odds rise. Every hero in the chamber does this on their own turn. Leaving is ordinary movement with no draws.
17. **Dragon wakes.** Every hero in the chamber resolves clockwise starting with the one who woke it: return all treasure-chamber cards to the bottom of that deck, take D12 damage, then if alive move to an adjacent labyrinth tile with no room card. When everyone has left, all used dragon cards are shuffled back in.
18. **Cards after use.** Used cards go to the bottom of their own deck unless kept. A "shuffle again" card effect makes used cards available again. Cards can chain to another deck (room to chest, room to search), grant kept treasure or useful objects with later conditional uses, start a monster encounter, or impose a trap.
19. **Victory.** The rulebook speaks of getting back to any tower before sunset. The Escape / Stay / Continue prompt and "inside at sunset" are owner rulings (D32-D34), not printed rules, and must not be "corrected" from the PDF.
