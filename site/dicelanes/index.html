          Rules

          The sheet

          • Four lanes: red and yellow run 2 to 12, green and blue run 12 to 2.
          • Cross numbers off from left to right only. Anything you skip is gone for good.
          • The last number in a lane (12 or 2) needs five crosses in that lane first. Crossing it also takes the lock, one more cross, and closes that colour for everyone.

          A turn with digital dice

          1. The active player rolls six dice: two white, red, yellow, green and blue.
          2. Everyone, starting with the active player, may cross off the sum of the two white dice in any lane.
          3. Then the active player may also cross off one white die plus one coloured die, in that colour's lane.
          4. If the active player crossed nothing at all this turn, they take a penalty.

          Several players can lock the same colour with the white sum. It closes once everyone has chosen. A closed colour's die leaves the game.

          With your own dice

          Play the same turns at the table and tap what you cross. Tap a lock to close a colour another player locked away from this device.

          Scoring

          • Each lane scores by its crosses: 1, 3, 6, 10, 15, 21, 28, 36, 45, 55, 66, 78.
          • Each penalty is minus 5.
          • The game ends when someone takes a fourth penalty or two colours are locked.
