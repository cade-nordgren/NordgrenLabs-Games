        How to play
        • Each round the sun moves one step. After sun 26 it sets, and anyone still inside is lost.
        • On your turn, go one square. New squares are drawn and turned so their mark faces you.
        • Rooms, doors, portcullises and monsters ask for cards, tests on a D12 against your stats, or a choice.
        • Two squares in the middle hold the treasure chamber. Each turn there gives two secret cards, then a dragon card.
        • Escape through any tower. Of those who escape, the most gold wins.
