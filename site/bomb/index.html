            How to play

            Get rid of every card you hold, in your hand and on your table. The first player with no cards left wins.

            The deal

            • Everyone gets four cards face down that nobody sees, and nine more.
            • Pick four of the nine to lie face up on top of your face-down cards. The other five are your hand.
            • The rest of the cards are the deck.

            Who goes first

            • Everyone draws one card. The highest rank starts. Ace is high and 2 is low.
            • Suits don't count: players tied for highest draw again, only them, until one is highest.

            A turn

            • Play a card that matches or beats the top of the pile. On an empty pile, anything goes.
            • You may play several cards of the same rank at once, like two Queens.
            • While the deck lasts, draw back up to five cards after you play.
            • Can't play, or don't want to? Pick up the whole pile instead. The next player then starts a fresh pile.

            Special cards

            • 2 resets. Play it on anything. The next player may play any rank. The pile stays.
            • 10 bombs. Play it on anything. The whole pile leaves the game and you play again.
            • Four of a kind bombs. Four cards of one rank in a row on top of the pile, played together or over several turns, also clear the pile. Whoever adds the fourth plays again.
            • 7 and 8 are ordinary cards.

            The three phases

            1. Hand. Play from your hand while you hold cards.
            2. Face up. When the deck is gone and your hand is empty, play your face-up cards.
            3. Face down. When those are gone too, turn over one face-down card at a time without looking. If it can't beat the pile, it goes on the pile and you pick the whole pile up.

            Picking up puts you back on your hand. Your table cards wait until your hand is empty again.

            On one phone

            After you play, your cards lock. Tap Next turn and pass the phone; the next player taps Ready to play. After a bomb you keep the phone.
