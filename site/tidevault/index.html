        How to play

        • You start at the dock at the bottom of the map. It is the only way out.
        • Tap a lit square next to you, or use the arrow keys, to swim. Rooms appear as you enter them.
        • Each swim costs 1 tide, plus 1 more for every 4 weight you carry. If the tide runs out anywhere but the dock, the run is lost.
        • Deeper rooms hold richer relics and tougher creatures.
        • A creature blocks you until you fight it, slip past, or drop a relic as a decoy. In a fight round you each roll a die and add power; the lower roll loses the difference in breath. Beat it to take what it guarded.
        • Tests roll two dice. Slipping past uses Wits, prying a seal uses Grip.
        • Out of breath means the run is lost. Surface at the dock to score the value of your relics.
