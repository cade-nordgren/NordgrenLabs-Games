            Rules

            The sheet

            • Four lanes: red and yellow run 2 to 12, green and blue run 12 to 2.
            • Cross numbers off from left to right only. Anything you skip is gone for good.
            • The last number in a lane (12 or 2) needs five crosses in that lane first. It shares a cell with the lock: crossing it counts as two crosses and closes that colour for everyone.

            Who goes first

            Everyone rolls one die, on screen or with real dice (then tap what it showed). The highest goes first; if several tie for the highest, only they roll again. Play then goes round in seating order. Online, the game rolls for everyone when the host starts. Every new game rolls again.

            A turn with digital dice

            1. The active player rolls six dice: two white, red, yellow, green and blue.
            2. Everyone chooses at once, in any order. Tap a name to switch sheets.
            3. Anyone may cross off the sum of the two white dice in any lane.
            4. The active player may also cross off one white die plus one coloured die, in that colour's lane. The white sum counts first.
            5. Picks can change until that player taps End turn. A greyed number with a dashed ring is ruled out by a pick; tap it to swap it in. While a pick is waiting it shows which dice made it: a white-die cell for the white sum, an X in the lane's colour for a white plus that coloured die. Once that player ends their turn it becomes a plain X, and when everyone has ended their turn, the crosses go on.
            6. The active player may tick a penalty box at any time during the turn, and must end the turn with at least one cross or a penalty. Other players never take penalties.

            Several players can lock the same colour on one roll. A colour locked on a roll closes once everyone is done, and its die leaves the game.

            Online

            Two to eight players, each on their own phone, with digital dice. Picks stay hidden until everyone has ended their turn. If a phone has been away for a minute, anyone else can skip that player: they cross nothing, and a skipped roller takes a penalty.

            With your own dice

            Play the same turns at the table and tap what you cross. Tapping a lane's last cell (the 12 or 2 with the lock) before it has five crosses closes that colour as locked by another player away from this device; tap it again to reopen.

            The app can't see your dice, so it can't tell a white-sum cross from a white-plus-colour cross. Every tap puts down the plain X straight away, with no dice colours.

            Scoring

            • Each lane scores by its crosses: 1, 3, 6, 10, 15, 21, 28, 36, 45, 55, 66, 78.
            • Each penalty is minus 5.
            • The game ends when someone takes a fourth penalty or two colours are locked.

            Big lane picker
