      How to play
      • Everyone shares one bank. Each roll of two dice adds its total.
      • The first three rolls of a round are safe, and a seven is worth 70.
      • From the fourth roll on, a seven ends the round and empties the bank. Doubles double it.
      • Between rolls, anyone can bank: the bank is added to their score and they sit out until the next round.
      • Highest score after the last round wins.
