Bank

    Rounds

      How to play
      • Everyone shares one bank. Each roll of two dice adds its total to the bank.
      • The first three rolls of a round are safe, and a 7 is worth 70.
      • From the fourth roll on, a 7 ends the round and empties the bank. Doubles double it.
      • Between rolls, anyone can tap Bank to add the bank to their score. They sit out until the next round.
      • The round ends on a 7 or when everyone has banked. Highest score after the last round wins.
