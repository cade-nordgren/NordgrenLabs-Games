Saved games

    Heroes

    Copy the numbers from each hero card. Stats are 1 to 12.

    What this tracks
    • The sun starts on 1. The game ends when it reaches 27.
    • A stat test rolls a d12 and succeeds on a roll at or under the stat.
    • The guardian deck holds 8 cards, one of them waking. A wake empties the vault cards of every hero in the vault and deals each a d12 of damage, then the deck resets.
    • At the end, escaped heroes are ranked by gold. If nobody escaped, the castle wins.
